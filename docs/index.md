# Indian Compliance Calendar

**A free, open-source compliance calendar and reminder system for Company Secretaries, CFOs and compliance teams in India.**

It knows every recurring statutory deadline for listed companies (NSE / BSE, equity and debt), unlisted public companies, private limited companies and LLPs. It works out due dates from your real board-meeting, earnings-call and AGM dates, checks BSE to confirm each filing was actually made, and keeps reminding your team by e-mail and WhatsApp until every item is closed.

## Two ways to use it

1. **Web app** you run on any office PC, laptop or server (Node.js, no database server needed).
2. **Claude skill**: upload it to Claude and Claude installs, runs, explains, troubleshoots and extends the app for you in plain English.
