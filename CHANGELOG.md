# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — October 2026

First public release.

- Compliance master: 61 recurring + 19 event-based items across SEBI LODR (equity & debt), PIT, SAST, DP, ICDR,
  Companies Act 2013 / MCA, LLP Act and optional tax & labour.
- Event-driven due dates from board-meeting, earnings-call and AGM dates; working-day rules with holidays.
- Automatic BSE verification (announcements, Integrated Filing – Governance & Finance, Shareholding Pattern) with exact
  submission time and filing link; auto-detection of board-meeting and AGM dates.
- Hidden-browser fallback when BSE blocks non-browser requests; NSE retries.
- Daily e-mail reminders until filed, per-workspace sender mailbox, snooze; WhatsApp one-tap and Twilio.
- BSE / NSE / SEBI circular feeds with per-user unread tracking.
- Multi-user and multi-client workspaces, invites, join links, trial sign-up, plan limits and expiry; activity log.
- Packaged as a Claude skill (`SKILL.md`, references, scripts).
- Documentation: getting started, user guide, how it works, Claude skill guide, FAQ; MIT licence.
